Approval uses the universal Shared Work Approval, preserving PROJECT/TASK request compatibility. A new business subject requires an explicit requested_action. Owner read ports enforce tenant, organization, workspace, domain permission and current lifecycle. Request stores a SHA-256 snapshot of the persisted record; pricing includes items and budgets include lines. The snapshot is a change detector, not a signature or an inferred business assessment.

The requester cannot decide their own request. Material decision needs the existing approval decision permission and the owner division lead's domain write authority. EXECUTIVE, IT_ADMIN, agents and a broad Shared Work read permission never grant business decision authority. Material approval reads, relations, evidence and comments retain owner visibility. Executive Shared Work summary retains its supported Project/Task approval slice; business sources are consumed through owner overview ports.

PENDING, RETURNED, REJECTED and HELD cannot execute. APPROVED does not mutate business state. An explicit business transition locks the owner record and the scoped approval, checks subject/action/snapshot/lifecycle, marks consumption with actor/time/transition reference, runs owner validation, writes business state, appends audit and validates projections before commit. Any failure rolls back consumption, business changes and audit. Concurrent execution has one winner. Consumed approval cannot replay even if a record is restored to a compatible old state.

Finance preserves exact Decimal strings, overpayment/duplicate-payment checks, row/period locks, reconciliation matching and recorded-document checks. Budget material changes use separate action approvals. Reconciliation/tax closure remains the existing constrained internal record verification, without fund transfer or external submission. Month-close final authority remains blocked pending official close policy. No invented monetary threshold exists. IT production release continues to use existing Governance DecisionRef and IT/DIRECTOR authority rather than creating another release decision store.

GA records are explicit internal human records. Inventory condition/readiness is never inferred from absence of maintenance or requests. Inventory, handovers, maintenance and assessments are immutable evidence; none changes Identity, Finance or another source automatically. Facilities use an operational request lifecycle; completion requires recorded resolution notes.

Legal Review is a distinct legal assessment with explicit summary/result and recorded reviewer. It is separate from due diligence, business approval, signature and execution. ContractRevision is immutable and references an actual immutable Shared Work document version. A contract with revision evidence cannot be overwritten. Neither review completion, an approved document nor a revision activates or executes the contract.
